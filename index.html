Our association brings together people with diverse backgrounds and perspectives, we create spaces where people can playfully meet, exchange ideas, and build things together. We initiate and realize transdisciplinary projects, especially in the fields of video, audio, performance, theatre, public screenings, exhibitions, and related artistic and cultural forms of expression. At the heart of it all is the shared experience: creating together, learning from one another, thinking further through creative dialogue within an open, inclusive environment that takes social, sustainable, and cultural questions seriously.
